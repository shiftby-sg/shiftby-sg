# ShiftBy.sg Source-Code Executive Experience Audit

Audit date: 2026-06-12  
Scope: source-code review of `app/**`, `components/**`, `content/**`, metadata, navigation, layout, CTA structure, `sitemap`, `robots`, and `llms.txt`.  
Out of scope: code quality, implementation style, package management, infrastructure, generated files.

## Executive Summary

ShiftBy has a distinctive and unusually coherent doctrine: "Execution changes. Understanding preserves confidence." The site consistently reinforces that idea across pages, and the best pages make the enterprise problem recognizable: decisions outlive context, AI amplifies accountability gaps, knowledge fragments, and confidence weakens when understanding is lost.

The main conversion weakness is not the doctrine. It is the gap between perspective and proof. The site explains the idea well, but an enterprise buyer still has to infer what an engagement looks like, what happens after contact, what evidence proves the method works, and why ShiftBy should be trusted over larger advisory firms or AI boutiques.

Overall: strong awareness and interest, moderate trust and evaluation readiness, weaker conversion readiness.

Recommendation handling: findings intentionally cross-reference a single prioritized recommendation register near the end of this report. When multiple sections expose the same issue, the recommendation is not repeated as a separate action.

## Bottom Line

ShiftBy already has a strong doctrine and a serious enterprise point of view. The website is most effective at creating recognition and interest. It is less effective at helping an executive evaluate buying risk.

The fastest path to higher conversion is not more doctrine. It is proof, process, and engagement clarity:

1. What happens after contact.
2. What an engagement looks like.
3. What the buyer receives.
4. What evidence shows ShiftBy can do this.
5. Why the founder is credible enough to trust.

If those are added without diluting the doctrine, the site can move from strong thought-leadership presence to credible executive advisory conversion system.
